# CoralConnect demo script

**Length:** about 2:30.
**Voice:** one presenter, to camera. A second person only appears when collaborate needs two phones.
**On screen:** the reef on a laptop, the admin page, and the phones. Cut the waiting. Keep the taps, the prompt, the receipt, and the reef moving.

| Time | Section |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:35 | The damage you can't see, and the people we bring with you |
| 0:35–1:30 | The two modes |
| 1:30–2:05 | One round, played |
| 2:05–2:30 | The environment, then the booth |

---

## 1. Open (0:00–0:35)

You, to camera. A chat window can sit behind you, then cut to the reef.

**YOU:** We created CoralConnect because your prompting has real environmental damage that you can't see. And we want to show it to you.

**YOU:** A short prompt can send the model looking through a whole document. That search is tokens, electricity, and a reef that pays for it. On the laptop, you watch the water take the hit.

**YOU:** But doing this alone is kind of sad, isn't it? So we grab some people to do it with you. You protect the reef next to someone, or you find out who protects it better.

---

## 2. The product (0:35–1:30)

Cut to the laptop. Admin page, then a phone.

**YOU:** We have two modes. Compete, and collaborate.

**YOU:** In compete mode, you start any game from the admin page. Pick a challenge, open the reef on the laptop, and the QR is the join link. Each game holds at most ten people. Your task is simple: solve the challenge with the most energy-efficient prompt you can write. Name the fact the model would otherwise go find. Leave it out, and that lookup is about twelve hundred tokens, and the reef wilts. Paste the whole file, and your own tokens count too. After you send, the phone prints a receipt: what you included, what the model had to look up, and the carbon. Ten friends, one reef, and you can see who spent the least.

Show the admin page, the QR, a phone joining, and the reef. One challenge card is enough to flash: the farm, the reef station, the cafe cooler, or the home battery.

**YOU:** Collaborate is how we grab someone to do it with you. There is no host and no room code. You open the pair room and answer two taps: what you build, and what you care about. We pair you with someone already waiting who overlaps with you. Both phones show the same animal, so you have to find that person in the room. Grok tells you what you share. Then each of you holds a different piece of the same challenge. You have two minutes. The prompt only works if you talk, because your partner's half is not on your screen. You share one score, and the reef is still the judge.

Show two phones. Tap Apps and Planet on both. Hold on the animal. Cut to the two of you finding each other, then to the two different jobs on the two screens.

---

## 3. Play one round (1:30–2:05)

Stay in collaborate, since both people are already on camera. Team chat is the round the pair room opens on.

**YOU:** Here's the damage you can't see, until we show it.

Type and send:

> Build the chat.

The reef wilts. Cut to the receipt.

**YOU:** It went looking. The receipt is the search. The reef is the ocean paying for it.

Then the careful prompt, said out loud while it is typed. You name your file. Your partner names theirs.

**YOU:** In messages.py, add send_message. Save the message and return it. In presence.py, add who_is_here. Return the people in that room.

Send. The receipt comes back inside the budget. The reef clears. If a turtle or a bloom appears, hold on it.

**YOU:** We said the file. We said the function. We said it together. The water stays alive.

---

## 4. Close (2:05–2:30)

Same person as the open, to camera. The reef is healthy behind you. The pair board can be the last shot.

**YOU:** And that's right. We can do a lot more to save the environment. It starts with education. Once you can see what a prompt costs the water, you can spend less of it. That's the game. The reef is the lesson.

**YOU:** Come find us at the booth. Bring a friend. See if you are the best at protecting the reef.

End card:

**CoralConnect**
A game of prompt battling, scored by the ocean.
coralconnectgt.tech

---

## Shoot notes

- Compete can be a short tour of the admin page. You do not have to finish a ten-person round on camera. Collaborate is the round you actually play.
- Have the reef full before "Build the chat," so the wilt reads.
- The animal and Grok's introduction are whatever the phone shows that take. Read those if you want the line on camera. The script does not depend on the exact wording.
- Team chat deals `messages.py` / `send_message` and `presence.py` / `who_is_here`. If the careful prompt only fills one file, send the second line as a follow-up.
